The Bone Runner is a 2D action platformer set in a dark dungeon.

Run, jump, and fight your way through skeleton warriors. Watch every step — spike pits, slicers, and hanging traps will end the run if you get careless.

Features:
- Fast 2D combat against skeleton enemies
- Deadly traps: spikes, slicers, and more
- Jump, attack, and stay on the move
- Pixel-art dungeon atmosphere
