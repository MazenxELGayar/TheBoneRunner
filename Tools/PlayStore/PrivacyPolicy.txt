Privacy Policy — The Bone Runner
================================

Last updated: 27 August 2026
Developer: MazenX

This policy applies to the Android game The Bone Runner (package com.mazenx.thebonerunner).

What the game is
  A single-player 2D game. You fight skeletons, avoid traps, and try to win.

Data we collect
  The Bone Runner does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases, ads, or analytics SDKs from us.

  The game does not collect your name, email, or contacts.

Google Play
  Google Play may collect standard install and crash information under Google’s own policies when you download or update the app from Play.

Device permissions
  The game does not need contacts, microphone, camera, or location.

Children
  The game is a general-audience action platformer. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes
  If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top.

Contact
  MazenX — use the developer contact email on the Google Play listing.
